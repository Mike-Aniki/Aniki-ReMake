<h2 align="center">Get Started</h2>

Aniki ReMake is a Fullscreen theme for [Playnite](https://playnite.link/) and uses **Aniki Helper** as its companion plugin.

Before installing, make sure you have:

- a current version of [Playnite](https://playnite.link/);
- Windows 10 or Windows 11;
- [Aniki Helper](https://playnite.link/addons.html#AnikiHelper_96a983a3-3f13-4dce-a474-4052b718bb52).

Other Playnite extensions are optional and only required for the additional features you choose to use.

<h2 align="center">About Aniki ReMake</h2>

**Aniki ReMake** is a highly customizable Fullscreen theme for Playnite built around one simple idea:

<div align="center">

### **Make a gaming PC feel natural to use like a console.**

</div>

It is designed primarily for televisions, controllers, living-room setups, and anyone who wants to launch Playnite and stay inside a controller-friendly interface instead of constantly returning to the Windows desktop.

Aniki ReMake goes beyond changing the appearance of the game library. The goal is to create a complete Fullscreen environment around your games and the way you use your gaming PC.

---

<h2 align="center">🎮 Designed Around the Controller</h2>

Aniki ReMake is built first and foremost for **Fullscreen navigation**.

Browse your library, open Game Details, switch views, use Quick Access, access additional features, and navigate secondary screens without constantly reaching for a keyboard or mouse.

Controller-oriented tools such as shortcuts, the virtual keyboard, gamepad mouse, Web Browser, and In-Game Overlay help keep you inside the Fullscreen experience.

<div align="center">

**Sit down. Pick up your controller. Play.**

</div>

---

<h2 align="center">More Than a Game Library</h2>

The **Welcome Hub**, **Quick Access**, **Extras**, and secondary views extend Playnite beyond the normal game list.

Achievements, captures, Steam features, news, music, applications, Video Center, browser tools, statistics, and other supported features can all become part of the same controller-friendly environment.

<div align="center">

**Spend more time inside your gaming interface and less time returning to the Windows desktop.**

</div>

---

<h2 align="center">Powered by Aniki Helper</h2>

<div align="center">

**[Aniki Helper](https://github.com/Mike-Aniki/AnikiHelper) is the heart of the Aniki ReMake experience.**

It powers the advanced features, integrations, menus, customization, and controller-focused tools that take Aniki beyond the limits of a traditional Playnite theme.

### **Aniki ReMake is the interface. Aniki Helper brings it to life.**

</div>

<h2 align="center">Make It Yours</h2>

<div align="center">

Aniki ReMake is designed to adapt to the experience **you** want.

Keep it clean and simple or build a richer console-style environment with your own visual identity, layouts, media, animations, and additional features.

### **There is no single Aniki setup — make it yours.**

</div>

<h2 align="center">The Story Behind Aniki</h2>

Aniki ReMake started with simple modifications to existing Playnite themes and gradually became something much larger.

From **Aniki Remix** to **Aniki ReMake**, and later **Aniki Lite**, the project grew through experimentation, community feedback, and a lot of trial and error.
